# Aura terms of use

Aura is a private, single-user, self-hosted dashboard for its owner's Oura data. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by Oura Health Oy. The owner must authorize access to Oura Cloud API V2 and comply with Oura's applicable terms. Oura's service and a qualifying membership may be required for data to appear.

Aura provides informational wellness displays, not medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. Measurements can be delayed or unavailable. The owner is responsible for securing their Mac, PC, local account credentials, and backups. Oura API access can be revoked through the Oura account.

This page describes this personal integration and does not grant rights to redistribute Oura data or use Aura as a public service.
